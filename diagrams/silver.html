WeatherTransformJob
com.cornbelt.silver
Transform steps
1
Load full bronze pool; split into anchor, supplemental, and all-station views
2
Compute pool median TMAX/TMIN via percentile_approx across all stations by date
3
Parse anchor attributes → suspect boolean; aggregate quality per stationId / date
4
Pivot anchor long → wide (TMAX, TMIN, PRCP, SNOW, SNWD)
5
Pivot supplemental → wide (TMAX, TMIN only)
6
Left-join anchor + supplemental + pool medians on date
7
Apply 3-level TMAX/TMIN: coalesce(anchor, poolMedian, supplemental); derive tempSource / tempStationId
8
Convert metric → US customary: °C × 9/5 + 32 = °F; mm / 25.4 = in
9
Parse date String → DateType; derive month and season
10
Compute 9 Boolean crop phase flags (corn / soybean / wheat × planting / growing / harvest)

Annual aggregates (groupBy year)
growingDegreeDays sum(gdd) Apr–Nov
totalPrecipIn sum(coalesce(precipIn,0))
frostFreeDays count(tempMinF > 32)
extremeHeatDays count(tempMaxF > 95)
avgTempF avg(tempAvgF) Apr–Nov
totalSnowIn sum(coalesce(snowIn,0))
Crop phase breakdowns (per crop × phase)
GDD, precipIn, avgTempF, snowIn, frostFreeDays, heatStressDays, droughtIndex computed for each of 9 crop×phase combinations (corn / soybean / wheat × planting / growing / harvest).
Same metrics computed for all 12 calendar months.
Drought index
(windowPrecip - meanPrecip) / meanPrecip
Computed for every precip window (general season, each crop×phase, each month).
Unbounded window — mean across all years in dataset.
